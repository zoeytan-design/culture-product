Cultural Heritage

From old-house tiles to modern glass objects.

Traditional Taiwanese floral tiles often appear in old houses, carrying memories of home, craftsmanship, and local visual culture. Tile & Trace does not directly reproduce these tiles. Instead, it extracts their floral motifs, symmetry, borders, and nostalgic colors, then reinterprets them through frosted translucent glass.
